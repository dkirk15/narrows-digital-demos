About the practice

Serving the Gig Harbor and surrounding community since 1991 — based only on public materials.

Simpson & Coyner Family Dentistry sits at 7715 Pioneer Way in Gig Harbor. Drs. Richard Coyner and Hunter Simpson pride themselves in offering gentle and compassionate dental care. When you visit, you are like family.

The doctors provide a full range of dental services ranging from routine exams and teeth cleanings to complex treatment plans and procedures. They welcome patients of all ages, treat conservatively, and especially enjoy both young and senior patients.
