Checkups & exams

A checkup is a routine exam of the teeth, gums, and mouth. The dentist looks for cavities and other common concerns and talks with you about what they see.

Professional cleanings

A professional cleaning removes plaque and tartar from the teeth, including places that are hard to reach with a toothbrush at home, and then polishes the teeth.

Fillings

A filling repairs a tooth after a cavity is cleaned out. An existing filling can also be replaced or repaired when it is cracked, worn, or loose.

Clear aligners

Clear aligners are a series of removable, transparent trays worn over the teeth to move them. Invisalign is one brand of clear aligners.
