About the practice

South Hill family dentistry since 2011 — dentist notes summarized from the public Meet Our Dentists page only.

Puyallup Family Dentistry is at 5620 112th St E, Suite 250 in Puyallup. Public materials describe high-quality, affordable dentistry for children, teens, and adults, and note that the practice has served Puyallup families since 2011.

The site brand is Puyallup Family Dentistry. Doctors are named below from the public Meet Our Dentists page — no invented biographies beyond that summary.

Christopher Carlson, D.D.S.

Grew up in southern Arizona; undergraduate education at Creighton University (where he met his wife, Rachel); doctorate from the University of Colorado School of Dental Medicine; general hospital residency at the University of Washington. Enjoys hiking, skiing, and the Pacific Northwest.

Jonathan Walker, D.M.D.

Practicing dentistry in Puyallup since 2009. Born and raised in Twin Falls, Idaho; biology degree from Brigham Young University–Idaho; doctorate from Case Western Reserve University School of Dental Medicine. Wife Caprice (Edgewood); family of five boys. Founded Walker Family Dental on South Hill in 2015. Member of the Pierce County Dental Society and the ADA. Enjoys working out, running, Spartan races, piano, and saxophone.

Nicole Ters, D.D.S.

Colorado native; graduated from the University of Colorado School of Dental Medicine. Completed Advanced Education in General Dentistry and public-health clinic work on the Big Island of Hawaii before recently moving to Washington. Enjoys the outdoors with her two dogs.
