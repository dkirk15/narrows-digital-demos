Services

Professional oral care and dental services for the entire family, from pediatric dentistry to cosmetic dentistry.

  • Family & preventive
  • Restorative
  • Cosmetic
  • Comfort & sedation
  • Emergency care

Family & preventive

  • Cleanings and deep cleanings

    Routine cleanings, plus deeper cleanings below the gumline when needed.

  • Pediatric dentistry

    Dental care for children.

  • Periodontal (gum) care

    Care for the gums and the tissue that supports your teeth.

  • Digital photography and X-rays

    Digital images and X-rays used to examine your teeth.

  • Treatment of TMJ disorders

    Care for problems with the jaw joint (TMJ).

Restorative

  • Tooth-colored fillings

    Fillings matched to the color of your teeth.

  • Crowns and bridges

    A crown covers a damaged tooth; a bridge replaces a missing tooth.

  • Inlays / onlays

    Custom-made restorations that fit into or onto a tooth.

  • Root canals

    Treatment for the inside (pulp) of a tooth.

  • Dental implants

    A post that replaces the root of a missing tooth.

  • Custom dentures and partial dentures

    Removable replacements for missing teeth.

  • Extractions

    Removing a tooth.

  • Wisdom teeth removal

    Removing wisdom teeth.

Cosmetic

  • Veneers

    Thin shells bonded to the front of teeth.

  • Teeth whitening

    Whitening treatment for your teeth.

  • Invisalign clear aligners

    Clear, removable aligners used to straighten teeth.

Comfort & sedation

We offer a variety of sedation options to make your experience as comfortable as possible.

  • Nitrous oxide

    Inhaled sedation, sometimes called laughing gas.

  • Oral conscious sedation

    Sedation taken by mouth before your visit.

Emergency care

We offer emergency service to patients who need it — call the office first: (253) 383-4901.
