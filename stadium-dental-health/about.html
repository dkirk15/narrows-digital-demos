About

Stadium Dental Health is at 2 N Tacoma Ave, Tacoma, WA 98403.

Professional oral care and dental services for the entire family, from pediatric dentistry to cosmetic dentistry, in a comfortable and caring environment.

We pride ourselves in taking every step possible to provide pain-free dentistry.

The office is kid-friendly. Staff are trained to care for nervous children, and there is a play area with toys and books.

Complimentary coffee, juice or tea is available at the beverage station.

Cleanliness and sterilization follow ADA and OSHA standards.

You can tour the facility and meet the doctors and staff.
