Personal injury

The clinic’s public materials say it treats personal injury claims, including motor vehicle accidents. This page is the request path for that care.

What is published

Olympic Occupational Medicine states that it evaluates and treats personal injury, including people hurt in motor vehicle accidents, alongside work injuries and occupational disease. This preview does not add treatment timelines, legal advice, or a fee schedule.

If the injury happened on the job, start on the injured-worker path so the reason for your callback matches.
