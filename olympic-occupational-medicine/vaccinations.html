Vaccinations

Employers can ask this clinic about workplace vaccinations. The public site title names vaccinations. It does not publish a vaccine list or prices.

What is published

Olympic Occupational Medicine’s public website title includes vaccinations. The pages that are actually published do not list vaccines, doses, or fees. This preview does not fill that gap with a made-up menu.
