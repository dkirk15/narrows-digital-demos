Injured workers

The clinic states that it evaluates and treats work injuries and occupational disease, including injuries and illness tied to the job. Request a callback or call the office.

What the clinic says it treats

Public materials describe care for people hurt on the job, occupational disease that develops from job demands, and exposure-related injury or illness. Workers’ compensation claims from around Western Washington are part of that published experience. This preview does not collect a clinical history.

  • Work injury evaluation and follow-up
  • Occupational disease related to job requirements
  • L&I and other workers’ compensation questions — confirm the details with the office

Leave a voicemail with your name, date of birth, and reason for calling. The clinic returns those calls the next business day.

How a callback works

  1. 1

    Call or send a request

    Use 253-649-0430 or the appointment form. Pick “Work injury / L&I” as the reason.

  2. 2

    Leave three facts

    Name, date of birth, and the reason for the call. Save the clinical story for the conversation with the clinic.

  3. 3

    Expect the next business day

    Monday through Friday, 9:00am–5:00pm. Closed weekends and federal holidays.
