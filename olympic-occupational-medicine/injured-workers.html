Injured workers

Care for injuries that happen at work — or occupational disease that develops over time from job demands. Request a callback or call the clinic.

What you can request

Public clinic materials describe evaluation and treatment for work injuries and occupational disease, including Western Washington workers’ compensation claims. Details of your claim and care plan are handled with the clinic — this preview does not collect clinical history beyond a short message.

  • Work injury evaluation and follow-up
  • Occupational disease concerns related to job requirements
  • Coordination context for L&I / workers’ compensation (confirm with the office)

Leave a voicemail with your name, date of birth, and reason for calling. The clinic typically returns calls the next business day.
