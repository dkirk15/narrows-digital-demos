Providers

BT

Brandon Turnbo, FNP

Family nurse practitioner · Occupational medicine

Certified family nurse practitioner specializing in occupational medicine, with published experience managing work injuries, occupational diseases, and workers’ compensation claims across Western Washington. Background includes post-acute care and in-house attending work at nursing homes in the Puget Sound region. He is participating in Medabra Foundation research on workers’ compensation policy and outcomes. Born and raised in West Tennessee; moved to Port Orchard in 2019 with his family. Outside the clinic: travel and competitive chess.

AANP · ACOEM · COHE Alliance of Western Washington

EB

Emma Broussard, AGNP

Adult-gerontology nurse practitioner

Certified adult-gerontology nurse practitioner, originally from Houston. She worked in oncology and hematology before occupational medicine, and previously built an inpatient palliative and pain program at a hospital in Central Texas, including sickle cell education. Nursing experience includes medical-surgical, acute care, alcohol and drug withdrawal, urology, hospice, and emergency departments. Active with the Altrusa Gig Harbor chapter. Enjoys hiking and kayaking.

SC

Sarah Coughlin, FNP

Family nurse practitioner

Certified family nurse practitioner, originally from New Jersey. She moved to the Pacific Northwest in 2019. Prior nurse practitioner work was in a hematology-oncology practice and an acute care clinic. She began as a Navy registered nurse in emergency and intensive care, with two deployments to Afghanistan. Outside the clinic: family, sports, and travel.

JA

Jolene Aden-Else, FNP

Family nurse practitioner

Certified family nurse practitioner with nearly three decades of care across geriatrics, home health, and family medicine, now focused on occupational health. Originally from Minnesota and most recently relocated from Texas to the Pacific Northwest. She lives with her husband, son, and pets, and is a marathon and ultramarathon runner with nearly 50 long-distance events.
